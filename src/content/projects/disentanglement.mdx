## Overview

Walking along a direction in a GAN's latent space should ideally change a single attribute. In practice, directions are often entangled: a direction meant for one attribute can drag others along with it. This work provides linked, interactive views — attribute charts, a regression view, and a projection of latent codes — for assessing how well a direction isolates the attribute it's meant to control, walk by walk.

The demo above is the live Observable notebook that accompanies the paper.
