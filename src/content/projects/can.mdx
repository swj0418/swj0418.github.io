## Overview

Two models can reach similar accuracy while representing the world very differently. CAN compares neural networks at the level of **concept-aligned neurons** — units whose activations line up with human-interpretable concepts — so that similarities and differences between models can be inspected visually rather than summarized by a single score.

Published in *Computer Graphics Forum* (EuroVis 2024), with Mingwei Li, Shusen Liu, and Matthew Berger.
