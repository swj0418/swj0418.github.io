## Overview

Transfer functions decide what a volume rendering shows, but tuning them means navigating a large, unintuitive parameter space. This work lets users describe what they want to see in **plain language** instead.

It combines **language–vision models** with a **fully differentiable rendering pipeline** and image-based losses guided by the text description, so a transfer function can be optimized directly toward the user's intent — making volume rendering more accessible to people who aren't visualization experts.

With Jixian Li, Shusen Liu, Chris R. Johnson, and Matthew Berger.
