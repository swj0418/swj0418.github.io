## Overview

During a research internship at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (January–August 2023), I built a scalable visual analytics system for exploring **80,000+ diffusion-generated candidate material structures**, enabling domain scientists to triage and prioritize outputs that would otherwise require manual review.

I partnered with materials science researchers to design the generative-model-driven discovery workflow end-to-end — from sampling to expert-in-the-loop evaluation.
