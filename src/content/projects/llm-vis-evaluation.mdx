## Overview

An end-to-end evaluation framework for LLM-generated Vega-Lite visualizations: prompt construction, structured-output parsing, schema validation, rendering, and feature-atom extraction.

I use it to quantify how raw data, metadata, summaries, and stated goals shape an LLM's design choices across thousands of generations.
